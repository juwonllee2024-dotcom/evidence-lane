# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and privacy-conscious project.

Expected behavior includes:

- be constructive and assume good intent;
- protect the privacy of people represented in evidence;
- discuss ideas and code, not people;
- accept correction without hostility.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, secret sharing, and deliberate exposure of personal data are not acceptable. Project maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this standard.
